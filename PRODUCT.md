# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Costa Rican freelance and independent developers — RJ and peers. They arrive with a specific dreaded question (CCSS retroactivity, IVA on exported services, TRIBU-CR filing), usually after work hours, wanting a straight answer they can verify. Secondary audience: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating the engineering through the product; they get the English shell and demo, not translated answers.

## Product Purpose

A RAG assistant that answers CR tax and trámite questions in plain Spanish, with every answer cited to the official document and artículo it came from. It retrieves and cites; it never rules. Success: a dev gets a grounded answer with a verifiable citation in under a minute, and the eval harness proves groundedness in CI. It is also the portfolio flagship — a public app with real backend, auth, and a nameable AI claim.

## Positioning

The only place where the answer to a CR freelance-tax question arrives with the exact artículo that backs it — cross-agency (Hacienda + CCSS), in the language developers actually ask in.

## Brand Personality

Técnico, eficiente, dev-first — executed through the visual world of the notaría: ink, folios, and the sello. The stamp is the brand: authority borrowed from paper, precision supplied by engineering. Warmth lives in the language (usted, plain Spanish, no jargon walls), never in decoration. The interface behaves like a sharp colleague who read the law so you don't have to.

## Anti-references

- Government portals (Hacienda/TRIBU-CR chrome, dense bureaucratic layouts, institutional blue).
- Generic AI chatbots (purple gradients, sparkles, glassmorphism).
- Fintech navy-and-gold trust clichés.
- Cutesy mascot apps — no cartoon accountants, no illustration crutch.

## Design Principles

1. **The citation is the interface.** Every design decision serves the moment an answer meets its source. The sello (citation chip) is the most crafted object on screen.
2. **Authority from the record, not from the app.** Tramitico never dresses up as an authority; it points at one. Provenance visible, disclaimer present, no ruling voice.
3. **Precision is the personality.** Dev-first shows as density, alignment, tabular numbers, and restrained motion — not as terminal cosplay.
4. **Subtle tico.** Costa Rica lives in the name treatment (trami·tico), the usted voice, and the trámite-world motif — never in flags or clichés.
5. **Both themes are first-class.** Light and dark are designed together; neither is an inversion of the other.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, keyboard-complete flows, visible focus states, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation. Long-form legal prose gets a readable measure and generous line-height in both themes.
